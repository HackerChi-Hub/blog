Collecting a book, a long video, or a podcast often results in not learning but lying into "I'll read later." Cangjie Skill aims to solve this gap: not just compressing content, but organizing reusable methods, processes, and judgment rules into skills that Agents can call based on scenarios.

## What it delivers is not a summary

Cangjie Skill is designed for books, long-form video transcripts, podcast transcripts, courses, interviews, long articles, and resource collections. It first forms a capability source and then compiles it into installable skill products; The project explicitly excludes ordinary book excerpts, book reflections, and author role-playing.

You can think of a regular summary as a courier SMS: it tells you roughly what's in the package. The Cangjie Skill is more like sorting goods onto a shelf, then marking which tasks to go to which location, how to use them, and when it's not applicable. The focus is not just on "remembering content," but on having the Agent find and execute the methods in subsequent tasks.

- Global understanding: generate BOOK_OVERVIEW.md that retains the main idea, structure, terminology, and critical analysis.
- Capability organization: Write validated methods into the Capability Bundle and capability cards, and record dependencies, comparisons, and combinations between capabilities.
- Installable products: can be compiled into single mode with a single routing entry point, or a pack mode with "routing entry + a few independent skills."
- Acceptance materials: Retain coverage audits, items to be verified, reasons for obsolescence, test cases, DIGEST.md, and operational records.

## How to start so you don't go off track right away

- Prepare the full text: it can be PDF, EPUB, TXT, subtitles, or transcripts. When there is no accessible text, the project requires stopping instead of generating from memory.
- Complete meta information: Books need title, author, and publication year; Videos, podcasts, or courses require titles, authors or speakers, and publication dates for traceability.
- Intended purpose: For learning and consulting, prefer singles; For daily workflows and cross-content combinations, packs are more preferred.
- The first time you try only one item: first confirm the extraction quality, call method, and test results, then decide whether to batch process.

The author's RIA-TV++ process first understands the entire material, then uses different extractors to find frameworks, principles, cases, counterexamples, and terminology; Candidate content is then checked for source adequacy, feasibility, and task gain, before deciding whether to become an independent skill or remain in a unified routing portal. Finally, representative task testing, compilation, and delivery are also performed.

## Who is worth trying?

- Suitable for: Those who already have a legally usable long text and want to integrate the methodology into the Agent workflow, rather than just a summary.
- Suitable for: Those willing to check sources, boundaries, trigger conditions, and test results, and willing to pilot with a single piece of material first.
- Not suitable for those who only have video links without subtitles or transcription and want tools to restore the entire content out of thin air.
- Not suitable for now: Those who only want to imitate the author's personality or plan to publicly distribute generated results without review.

The safest starting point is to choose a material with clear copyright boundaries, familiar to you, and able to verify the original text, and use a single-model small-scale pilot. During acceptance, don't just look at whether the file has been generated; randomly select several abilities to verify sources, trigger conditions, execution steps, failure boundaries, and actual task results. Only when all these match can the Cangjie Skill transform from a "document organization project" into a truly usable workflow.
