## What does 50 steps become 6 steps?

![Mechanism Illustration | Post-Training Process](https://hyphentech.top/obsidian-assets/pika-h3-flex-localbrain-mac/image-posttraining-7b32bd9b86.png)

Let's first clarify the "step." The audio-video generation model repeatedly corrects the image and sound in compressed representations, gradually moving from noise to finished images—a process often called denoising. It's like drawing a sketch: first determine the whole, then repeatedly adjust the shape and details. But here, each "step" is a model calculation, not a frame of video or a pixel.

So, 6 steps can generate 124 frames, which is completely uncontradictory. Frame count refers to how many consecutive frames are present in the final image; Step count refers to how many major updates the model has made to create this set of images and sounds. Mixing these two numbers together can mistakenly think 6 steps can only draw 6 images.

### Choose the right teachers first, then teach students who can avoid detours

Pika's publicly available method is divided into three training stages. First, internally selected audio and video are used for supervised fine-tuning to make the original 50-step model better meet the target in style and task performance; Next, distribution matching distillation is performed to reduce the generation process to 6 steps; Finally, reward and preference learning is used to improve the fast model's output.

"Distillation" can be understood as learning to cook: a master chef might taste and revise many rounds as they go, while apprentices are specially trained to achieve results close to the target after fewer rounds. The teacher and students in the model are not people guiding frame by frame on site; Training should make the results produced by students with fewer steps be distributed closer to the teacher's. It learns a shorter generation path, so you can't change the standard H3 setting from 50 to 6 and declare Flex.

The technical page states that ordinary distributed matching distillation has training instability issues in their experiments, so **PDMD, also known as projection distribution matching distillation**, is adopted. This name doesn't need to be memorized; readers should grasp: the six-step effect is trained, backed by specialized models and sampling schedules. Missing a few calculations is just the result; how to calculate less without obvious damage to the finished film is the real technical work.

### After doing it quickly, you also need to teach it what results are worth keeping

After distillation, Pika continues training using two preference methods. ReFL focuses more on aesthetics and clarity, while DPO uses paired preference to learn instruction compliance, realism, and visual quality. It can be understood as the chef tasting the finished dish after the food is served faster: not just asking "Is the dish ready?" but also "Does it look good? Is it done according to the menu?"

These two parts are trained and merged in the form of a low-rank adapter, i.e., LoRA. LoRA is like adding a set of smaller tuning parts to an existing large machine, so you don't have to rebuild the entire machine; But runtime still requires a base, and downloading the adapter itself doesn't create a complete generator out of thin air. The official statement says that the merged preference adapter does not increase the number of generation steps.

### Sound isn't enough; it also needs to be easier for you to edit

![Mechanism Illustration | Voice Division](https://hyphentech.top/obsidian-assets/pika-h3-flex-localbrain-mac/image-audio-stems-0b139e225c.png)

Flex generates synchronized stereo. Pika hosting service also allows extraction of five types of audio tracks: dialogue, soundtrack, special effects, foley, and ambient sound. The difference can be understood when watching TV at home: when the entire sound is mixed together, if you want to lower the background music, the voice often becomes quieter as well; After track splitting, you can tune only the music, leaving only dialogue and live effects.

The most easily confused here are "synchronized audio" and "independent tracks." This issue of the original Mac audio saves mixed stereo tracks that can be played or edited directly; The five types of independent tracks are official managed extensions, and currently the LocalBrain local access is not open. Using a sound MP4 cannot be directly considered as having already obtained five separate mixable materials.

## Where exactly does the cost of local openness lie?

Pika discloses the adapter and inference code, which must be paired with the specified base. The official original sample is designed according to NVIDIA's single SIM request, with acceptance environments including the H100 80GB; CPU uninstallation reduces GPU phase usage but incurs memory and handling costs. CPU uninstallation can be understood as insufficient kitchen countertops, placing some ingredients on the nearby shelves: the countertop is free, and retaking and retrieving is not free.

Some uninstall configurations in the official documentation show GPU phase about 29~32GiB, while host observations show about 94GiB including page cache. Therefore, "VRAM can be reduced to 32GB" and "a 32GB computer can run" are not the same statement. My Mac results come from another Apple chip MLX/Q8 adaptation, which cannot be directly copied from NVIDIA commands or directly applied to all Mac models.

The 8 in Q8 refers to the main weights represented by about 8 bits. You can think of it as reducing decimal places for a large number of measurements: file and storage load may decrease, errors may be introduced as a result, and the actual operation depends on which parts are converted and how they run. This time, we used a dedicated backbone and adapter combination for the receiver, not just changing the suffix of any H3 file.

For Coffee Galaxy, Rainy Night Whale, Cardboard Deep Sea, first write the main subject and continuous actions, then look at the model's specific accomplishments. Keep what can be used, and note down odd and disobedient parts as usual.

Pika's approach this time is also worth observing: first train H3 post-generation into a generator with fewer steps and better suited for creative control, then add high-resolution, track-splitting, and acceleration services, and open up adapters for developers to create their own tools. This layering gives local tools a chance to absorb some capabilities, while leaving commercial services to refinement and the complete production chain. Whether it can attract creators long-term depends on modification success rate, product stability, and total cost of the entire process.

> [!quote] My judgment
> The value of the Pika H3 Flex lies in the six-step audio generation through dedicated post-training, with continued creative control based on references and modifications. For my 64GB Mac, the actual results so far are: using LocalBrain, I got a 640×384 5-second creative video with original sound in about three minutes. I started with this already successful small task, then decided whether I needed cloud multi-reference, track splitting, and 4K.
