Free AI Toolbox — 64 personally tested recommendations

AI Tools · Personal Tests and Recommendations · 8 Major Categories · Free Quotas · VPN Explanations · Recommended to Bookmark

It’s 2026, and if you haven’t started using AI tools in your daily work, you’re really wasting your time.

The question isn’t ‘whether to use it,’ but 'which one to use—there are tons of recommendations online, but which ones are truly free? Which ones require VPN access? Is the free version enough?

I spent two months personally testing 64 AI tools, organizing this list into 8 major scenarios. Each tool is marked with free limits, whether you need a VPN, and what suits you best.

All information is based on actual testing in April 2026, and the free policy may be adjusted at any time.

This is the first step for most people to get involved with AI. Choosing the right chatbot can instantly double your writing efficiency.

5. AI Music Generation (2 models)

Writing a song is no longer just musicians’ domain. Enter a description, and AI will generate the complete song—with vocals and arrangement.

Tools Free quota VPN The most suitable choice
Suno 50 credits/day (about 10 songs) No need Songs in all styles, Chinese songs
Udio 10 credits/day Yes High-quality music and a variety of styles

Suno has excellent Chinese support, with 10 free tracks per day, more than enough for background music or entertainment. Udio’s sound quality is more refined but requires a VPN to use.

6. AI Translation (3 models)

Machine translation is no longer enough. AI translation understands context, and the translation quality approaches human level.

Tools Free quota VPN The most suitable choice
DeepL 500,000 characters per month No need Academic papers, professional translation
Immersive translation The basic version is free No need Bilingual webpage support and real-time translation
Tencent Translator 5 million characters per month No need Chinese scenarios and document translation

Install ‘immersive translation’ for English web pages, use DeepL for document translation, and use Tencent Translate for Chinese-related scenarios. Installing all three is still not a loss.

7. AI PPT / Office (4 models)

Making PPTs is the most time-consuming. AI can help you generate everything from outline to design with one click, saving 80% of the time.

Tools Free quota VPN The most suitable choice
Gamma 400 AI credits (about 10 PPT files) Yes One-click PPT generation with strong design sense
iFlytek Zhiwen The free version is available No need Chinese PPT and speech drafts are generated
iSlide The basic version is free No need PPT plugins and one-click layout
Beautiful.ai The basic version is free Yes Business presentations and automatic layout

For Chinese PPTs, iFlytek Zhiwen is the first choice—free and easy to use. For English presentations, use Gamma or Beautiful.ai, which has a stronger sense of design.

8. AI Search (3 models)

Traditional search engines give you a bunch of links, but AI search gives you the answer directly. This is a generational upgrade in search methods.

Tools Free quota VPN The most suitable choice
Perplexity 5 Pro searches per day Yes In-depth research brings source citations
Mita AI search Free and unlimited No need Chinese search and academic research
Quark Free of charge No need Daily search and document Q&A

In China, using Mita AI search is free and unlimited, and you can also search academic papers. If you need to search for English materials, use Perplexity, which offers the highest quality answers.
