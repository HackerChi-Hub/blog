Questions from readers

Compatibility and feedback: two starting points.

How do I choose between Intel Mac and Apple silicon downloads?

Check your chip in About This Mac, then read the platform and package details on the product page. Sharing macOS does not mean sharing a chip architecture. Ask before using a package that does not match your device.
