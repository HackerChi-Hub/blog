AI prompt template library

Prompt Engineering · Quick Template Quick Reference · Covering 12 Daily Scenarios · Direct Copy and Paste · Recommended to Bookmark

People who know how to use AI and those who don’t have five times the efficiency. The difference is—can you write prompts?

Today, I’ve compiled 12 of the most frequently used daily scenarios, each with 2-3 prompt templates that can be directly copied and pasted. Replace the content in curly brackets with your own, paste it into any AI dialog box, and it’s ready to use.

1. Write weekly reports / work reports

Template 1: STAR Weekly Report Generator

You are a seasoned career coach. Based on the scattered work content I provide, please organize it into a professional weekly report using the STAR rule (Scenario-Task-Action-Result).

Article writing: Character setting + word count + style + structure + audience

Requirements:

  1. Each task is summarized in one sentence, expressed in data (e.g., “Improved by X%” or “Saved Y hours”)
  1. It is divided into three sections: “Completed This Week,” “In Progress,” and “Next Week’s Plan.”
  1. Annotation priority (P0/P1/P2)
  1. The obstacles encountered and the support needed are listed separately
  1. Use a professional and concise tone to avoid a routine

Email Writing: Recipient + Purpose + Tone + Length

My job responsibilities are as follows: {{RAW_work_items}}

My position is: {{job_title}}

The reporting target is: {{report_to}}

The more specific the fragmented content, the better. For example, “fixing 3 bugs” is 10 times more effective than “doing development.”

Template 2: Monthly Summary + Highlights Extraction

You are a workplace consultant skilled in upward management. I need to write a monthly work summary to report to the leaders at the {{report_level}} level.

Below is my original work record for this month: {{monthly_raw_notes}}

Please help me:

  1. Extract 3-5 core highlights, each using a logic chain of “What was done→ how was done→ and what impact did it have?”
  1. Quantify all results that can be measured
  1. Package daily work as higher-dimensional expressions such as “projects/systems/mechanisms.”
  1. Next month, we plan to align with the company’s OKRs
  1. Keep it under 500 words

2. Create a PPT outline

Template 1: Business Report PPT Structure Generator

You are a McKinsey-level business presentation consultant. Please design a PPT outline for the following topics.

Presentation Topic: {{presentation_topic}}

Target audience: {{audience}} (Focus: {{audience_context}})

Demonstration duration: {{duration}} minutes

Purpose of Demonstration: {{purpose}} (Persuasion decision-making / Progress reporting / Training and teaching / Investment promotion roadshow)

Please output:

  1. One page of “Core Information Card”: Summarize the message you want your audience to take away in one sentence
  1. Complete page number planning (each page’s title + key points + suggested visual format)
  1. Organized according to the “Pyramid Principle”: conclusions first→ evidence supporting → action recommendations
  1. Label which pages need data charts and which need case stories
  1. Design an opening hook and a closing call to action

Template 2: Pitch Deck 10-page structure for the roadshow

You are a pitch coach helping startups raise funds. Please design a 10-12 page pitch deck outline based on Silicon Valley standards.

Project Overview: {{project_intro}}

Industry Track: {{industry}}

Financing stage: {{funding_stage}}

Financing amount: {{amount}}

Output is structured as follows: cover → problem → solutions→ product demo→ market size→ business model→ competitive landscape→ operational data→ team→ financial forecasts and financing plans

3. Translation and polishing

Template 1: Academic/Business Grade Chinese-English Translation

You are a professional translator fluent in both Chinese and English, with expertise in the domain of {{domain}}.

Please translate the following content as {{target_language}}:{{source_text}}

Translation Requirements:

  1. Do not translate word for word; convey the exact meaning and technical terminology of the original text
  1. Industry terminology reference: {{glossary}}
  1. Tone Style: {{tone}} (Formal Business / Academic Paper / Casual Speaking / Technical Document)
  1. A “Key Terminology Table” is included after translation.
  1. If there is ambiguity in the original text, mark it with [Note:…] and provide both versions

Template 2: Correction of Chinglish formality

You are a native English academic editor. Below is the English text I wrote; please help me polish it.

Original: {{english_text}}

Retouching Requirements:

  1. Corrected all grammatical errors, especially Chinglish expressions
  1. Enhance the precision and diversity of word choice
  1. Mark every modification and explain why it was changed
  1. Two versions available: “Conservative Version” + “Authentic Version”
  1. Text Purpose: {{purpose}} (academic papers/business emails/personal statements PS)

Universal prompt formula

The templates for the 12 scenarios above actually follow the same underlying formula:

Role: You are a {{Role}} with specialized knowledge in {{Field}}.

Task: Please help me {{specific task}}.

Background: {{Background Info}} + {{Constraint}}

Format Requirements: {{ Output Format 1}} + {{ Output Format 2}} + {{ Output Format 3}}

Example (optional): Input{{Example input}} → Expected output{{Example output}}

Priority of the five elements:

  • 🎭 Roles — Determine the AI’s level of knowledge and vocabulary style

  • 🎯 Tasks — The more specific, the better

  • 📋 Background — Give AI enough context

  • 📐 Formatting — Clearly define the output structure

  • 📝 Example — Give an expected output sample

Practical tip: If the AI is not satisfied with the first output, don’t rewrite the prompt—just say “Point 3 is too vague, expand on it” or “The tone is too formal, change it to conversational.” Multi-turn conversation > One perfect prompt.
