7. Prototype/UI tools

Engaged in app design, web prototyping, and prototype demonstrations.

Tools Explanation Free plan
Figma Industry-standard collaborative design platform, UI/UX/prototype Three free items
Penpot An open-source Figma alternative, supports CSS Grid, and can be self-hosted Open source and free of charge
Mockup World Lots of free sample templates (mobile/computer/packaging PSD) Free of charge
Smartmockups Online prototype generator, no PS required Free basic version

Figma is the industry standard; the free version with three projects is enough for personal use. If you care about privacy and data sovereignty, choose Penpot.
